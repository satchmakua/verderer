What a valid proof establishes

It does show

That Verderer’s collector received exactly these bytes, with this status, from this address; that the record of it is in the signed log; and — when the bundle carries an independent timestamp — that the log existed no later than that time.

It does not show

What the agency “really” published, what other visitors were served, anything before the first recorded response, or why something changed. More on the limits →

Verderer attests only what its collector received: this response, from this address, at this time — preserved faithfully and verifiable offline. Labels such as severity and change type are a best-effort, human-reviewable interpretation, never a verified fact. What is and isn't proven →
