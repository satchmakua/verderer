Reading this record

What a change is

Whenever a source answers differently than it did last time, the two versions are compared and whatever moved is listed — a watched term, a number with a unit, a passage, a column of data. One comparison is one change.

What is proven

That this collector received these exact bytes from this address at this time, and that the record has not been altered since. Severity and change types are interpretation, and labelled as such.

What “blocked” and “unreachable” mean

A blocked source refuses the collector, so its content is not being observed. An unreachable one has never answered, so there is nothing to record. Neither is hidden: an absence is part of the story.
